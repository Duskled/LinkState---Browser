About Browser

The web was meant to be shared, not complicated.

We are bypassing the servers, the terminals, and the deployment pipelines. By stripping away the steep learning curve of repositories and IDE setups, this environment puts the power of instant creation and seamless sharing back where it belongs: in your hands.

The End of Deployment Friction

Remember the hours lost explaining what a repository is, or wrestling with git push just to show someone a prototype? That ends today. Package your entire project into a single, instantly executable link or a lightweight .onestate file. You share it. They open it. It runs locally. It is an absolute lifesaver for beginners and a massive time-saver for professionals.

Your Private Digital Library

When you find a project you love, you shouldn't have to rely on a third-party server to keep it. Bookmark it directly into your local environment using a custom name.linkstate alias. You are building a permanent, offline, and instantly accessible archive of the web—entirely on your own terms.

The Paradigm Shift (Future Roadmap)

This is just the prototype. We are fundamentally rethinking how browsing, building, and sharing works:

  • Limitless Expansion: Breaking the boundaries of file size and format, moving far beyond standard HTML/CSS/JS to support massive, asset-heavy digital environments and multimedia.
  • The Ultimate Workspace: Why switch apps? Soon, you will write, edit, and execute your code directly inside the browser UI. The viewer becomes the studio.
  • The Compiled Web (Locked .onestates): The most disruptive shift in web sharing. Imagine packaging your project so it runs perfectly offline, but the source code is entirely locked. No decoding. No file extraction. Share your vision securely, protect your intellectual property, and deliver offline services with absolute confidence.
